ODL / RESEARCH TOOL 016
DOCUMENT ANALYSISPDF / AI Q&A

Ask your PDF.
Find the useful bits.

Upload a text-based PDF, let your browser extract its text, then ask a question. The extracted text is sent to the AI service only when you press the ask button. AI answers can be wrong, so check important details against the original document.

Text-based PDFs are supported. Scanned/image-only PDFs may need OCR and may not produce readable text. Up to 40 pages and 12,000 characters are sent for analysis.
