PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Important: This policy is a project draft and must be reviewed against the services actually enabled before public launch.

Text entered into tools

Most browser-based tools process input locally in your browser. If you choose an AI writing, summary or PDF question feature and submit it, the text you provide (and your PDF's extracted text for PDF questions) is sent to this site's server-side endpoint for processing by Cloudflare Workers AI. Do not submit passwords, API keys, sensitive personal data, confidential client information or other information you are not comfortable sending for AI processing. AI features may be unavailable if the service is not enabled.

Hosting and external resources

Cloudflare hosts this site and may process standard request data, such as IP address, browser details and access logs, under its own policies. When an AI feature is used, the submitted text is processed by the configured Cloudflare Workers AI service. The stylesheet may request fonts from Google Fonts. Review those providers' current privacy documentation for details about their handling of data.

Cookies and analytics

The current project code does not intentionally set tracking cookies or include analytics scripts. Hosting and third-party providers may use their own technical processes; this draft should be reviewed if analytics, forms or other services are added.
