PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Important: This policy is a project draft and must be reviewed against the services actually enabled before public launch.

Text entered into tools

Most browser-based tools process input locally in your browser. If you choose an AI writing, summary or PDF question feature and submit it, the text you provide (and your PDF's extracted text for PDF questions) is sent to this site's configured server-side endpoint for processing by its configured AI provider. Do not submit passwords, API keys, sensitive personal data, confidential client information or other information you are not comfortable sending for AI processing. AI features may be unavailable if the backend is not deployed or configured.

Hosting and external resources

The hosting provider configured for this site may process standard request data, such as IP address, browser details and access logs, under its own policies. When an AI feature is used, submitted text is processed by the AI provider configured for the site's backend. The stylesheet may request fonts from Google Fonts. Review the actual providers' current privacy documentation before launch and update this notice to match the services in use.

Cookies and analytics

The current project code does not intentionally set tracking cookies or include analytics scripts. Hosting and third-party providers may use their own technical processes; this draft should be reviewed if analytics, forms or other services are added.
